Board-first layout

Issue #422 makes a board its own thing in a lab: its own address, its own comments, found from ⌘K, opened on its own, and only shown on a page as a link. The issue already settles four things: phones get a full-screen board, boards get their own place in the lab list, a page shows a board only as a preview or a small marker, and board comments belong to the board. These five questions are what's left for you to decide about how it looks. Pin a number on each section (for example "2B"). The recommended one is outlined.

4. The board on a phone

Full screen is already decided. What's left is how you comment and draw without a keyboard. Today drawing means holding D, which a phone doesn't have.

4A · Draw in the top barrecommended

← backboard namedraw

The bar you know, plus one word. Tap a frame to pin it. "draw" switches drawing on until you tap "done". One finger pans unless drawing is on.

4B · Bar at the bottom

Tap pins, and holding a finger down then dragging draws. Nothing on screen, but nobody would find it, and it clashes with holding to select a frame.

6. A board as a pin on the page

This replaces 5. Nothing is written into the page. A board sits at the spot you picked like any comment pin, and its comments stay with the page. The pin shows when there's new activity on the board, the way a comment pin does today. Opening it brings up the board over the whole page (3B).

6A · Pin with the word "board"

board

A normal pin with "board" next to it. A tap opens the board straight away. New activity shows as the pin's usual dot.

6B · A text chip

board · needs your reply

No pin shape, just words at the spot that say what's waiting. A tap opens the board. Tells you the most at a glance, but it's a new kind of thing on the page.

the words you typed with +board

The usual pin and card. The card shows the board's pictures and an "Open board" button. New activity shows on the pin's usual ring. Reuses the pin, card and status there are now, with nothing new on the page.

Decided by the issue, not asked here

  1. Board comments move to the board, keeping their ids. There's a backup first, and running it twice changes nothing.
  2. Picking a frame never resolves its comments.
  3. Only one board is open and taking input at a time.
  4. The order of work: fix drawing and the phone sheets first, then boards in ⌘K and the full-window board, then importing several images at once, then page markers, then scenarios.
