Billing questions

When am I charged?

On the first of each month, for the seats in use that day.

Can I pay once a year?

Yes, and two months are free. You can change this at any time from the billing page.

Do you send invoices?

Every charge comes with a PDF invoice, sent to the billing contact.

Who is the billing contact?

The person who created the workspace. You can change this at any time from the billing page.
