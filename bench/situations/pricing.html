Questions

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel at any time from the billing page, and your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel within 30 days of your first payment we refund it in full, no questions asked.

What happens to my calendars if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Calendars above the Solo limit become read-only, and history older than 90 days is hidden — not removed — until you upgrade again. Guests you invited keep their access, but you can’t add new ones past the limit. Exports & backups keep working on every plan.

Do guests need an account?

No. Guests answer from the invitation email; they only need an account if they want to see your team’s shared availability.
